# Document OCR

Reads text from scanned PDFs and images so you can search, select, quote, and copy text from scans just like regular digital books.

<!-- more -->

## What You Can Do

- **Turn scans into searchable documents**: Automatically recognizes pages in scanned PDFs that don't have selectable text yet, or run **Recognise text** from a file's right-click menu at any time.
- **Select and quote from scanned pages**: Once recognized, you can highlight text right on the scanned page, copy quotes with links, and find the document in search.
- **Pick up where it left off**: Long books are saved every few pages as they are processed, so you never lose progress if you close the app.

## Settings

- **Model**: Choose the language script that matches your documents (such as Latin, East Slavic, Cyrillic, Devanagari, Greek, or Chinese/English), or choose **Custom model** to use your own model file.
- **Scan resolution**: Higher resolution (such as `400 dpi` or `600 dpi`) helps read small or faded print, while `300 dpi` is a fast everyday default.
