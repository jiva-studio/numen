# Audio/Video Transcription

Turns your audio and video recordings into interactive, time-stamped transcripts right on your computer.

<!-- more -->

## What You Can Do

- **Transcribe lectures, interviews, and voice notes**: Click **Transcribe** inside the Media Player or right-click any audio or video file in the sidebar to generate a full transcript offline.
- **Read along as you listen**: Each line of the transcript is linked to its timestamp—click any line to jump the recording to that moment, and search your recordings just like text notes.
- **Ask the agent to transcribe a recording**: The agent in the chat panel can start the transcription of a recording with the tool `transcript_create` and read the transcript as it is written.

## Settings

- **Model**:
  - **Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3** _(default)_: Supports 25 European languages and detects the spoken language automatically.
  - **Parakeet TDT 0.6B v2**: Optimized specifically for English recordings.
