The plex is the shape of your links. The files tab is the shape of your folders: the vault
exactly as it is on disk, with everything in it — notes, books, pictures, whatever else you keep
there.

Open one from a new tab, or with **Show the files of the vault** in the
[commands](/commands/). **Show this note in the files** finds the note you are reading in the
tree.

## Moving things

Drag a row into a folder. Several at once if several are chosen, and the pointer says how many
it is carrying. A folder goes whole, with everything filed inside it.

Two things are refused, quietly and safely:

- **A name already taken** in the folder you dropped on. That row stays where it was, the others
  go, and numen names the ones it left behind.
- **A folder dropped into itself**, or into anything under it.

Moving a note is a proper move: links that pointed at it are looked at, and the ones that would
otherwise reach nothing are [put right](/links/#after-a-rename-or-a-move).

## Renaming

**Rename** on a row puts the name into a field where it stands. For a note, the title follows
the filename — the new name is written into the note — unless you have
[told the two apart](/writing/#renaming) in the settings.

## Making things

**New note** and **New folder** make them in the folder you asked on, and the new folder opens
with its name in a field, waiting to be called something.

## What a row offers

Over a note, the menu is everything [the commands](/commands/) offer over one, in bands: open it
or show it in the plex; rename, copy its path, make a note beside it; make it a
[child, a parent or a jump](/links/); change its title; ask the agent about it; remove it.

Over anything else — a PDF, a picture, a file numen does not read — the menu is what can be
done to a file: rename it, copy its path, make something beside it, remove it.

Choose several rows and the menu offers what means something for all of them, which is removing
them.

**Remove** puts a file in `.trash/` inside the vault, the same as anywhere else in numen.
Nothing is destroyed.

## Carrying notes onto the map

Rows lift out of the tree: pick notes up and carry them over the rest of the window. Drop them
into a seat in the [plex](/plex/) — above, below, or to the side of a node — and they are
**linked** to that note, as parents, children or jumps.

Only notes are carried this way. A picture or a PDF among the chosen rows stays where it is,
because a link in the vault runs between notes.
