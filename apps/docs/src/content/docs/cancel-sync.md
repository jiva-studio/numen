---
title: Cancel Numen Sync
description: How to cancel the Numen Sync subscription, in the app or without it, and what happens to your vaults in the cloud afterwards.
---

Numen Sync is paid through Creem, which takes the payment and keeps the subscription. You cancel it in the app, or without the app on the Creem website with the email you paid with. Numen does not know that email and cannot cancel a subscription for you.

## In the app

1. Open the settings and go to **Identity and licenses**.
2. On the card **Numen Sync**, choose **Manage subscription**. The Creem customer portal opens in your browser.
3. Cancel the subscription in the portal.

The card shows the new state the next time it asks the sync server.

## Without the app

You can cancel without numen, for example when you lost this device and your 12 words:

1. Open [creem.io/my-orders/login](https://creem.io/my-orders/login).
2. Sign in with the email you used when you paid.
3. Find the Numen Sync subscription and cancel it.

The receipt Creem sends after each payment has a link to the same portal.

If you no longer have that email either, write to Creem support at [creem.io/contact](https://creem.io/contact). Numen stores no email and cannot find your subscription by one.

## After you cancel

Nothing more is charged. Your vaults are not removed from your devices; what changes is what the cloud does with them.

| When                                          | The card shows   | What happens                                                          |
| --------------------------------------------- | ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| until the end of the paid period              | **Active until** | sync works as before                                                  |
| the first 7 days after the period ends        | **Ended**        | changes are still sent to the cloud                                   |
| from then until 60 days after the period ends | **Read only**    | the vaults in the cloud can only be read; changes stay on this device |
| 60 days after the period ends                 | **Deleted**      | the cloud deletes your vaults; the notes on your devices stay         |

A trial you cancel ends at the end of the trial, with nothing charged.

Buy a subscription again on the same card before the cloud deletes your vaults, and sync goes on with them.
