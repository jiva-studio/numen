The plex shows your links. The files tab shows your folders: the vault
exactly as it is on disk, with everything in it, including notes, books, pictures and any other file you keep
there.

Open one from a new tab, or with **Show the files of the vault** in the
[commands](/commands/). **Show this note in the files** selects the note you are reading in the
tree.

## Moving things

Drag a row into a folder. You can drag several rows at once if several are selected, and the pointer shows how many
it is dragging. A folder moves whole, with everything inside it.

Two cases are rejected without changing anything:

- **A name already taken** in the folder you dropped on. That row stays where it was, the others
  go, and numen lists the ones it did not move.
- **A folder dropped into itself**, or into anything under it.

When you move a note, numen checks the links that pointed to it and [fixes](/links/#after-a-rename-or-a-move) those that would
otherwise point to nothing.

## Renaming

**Rename** on a row opens an inline editable text field for the item. For a note, the title follows
the filename — the new name is written into the note — unless you have
[made them separate](/editor/#renaming) in the settings.

## Making things

**Create note**, **New deck**, **New card model**, **New preset** and **Create folder** create them in the folder you chose, and the new folder opens with a field where you type its name. A deck and a card model are [cards](/cards/); a preset is [how much a day of review holds](/review/#a-preset).

## What a row offers

For a note, the menu has everything [the commands](/commands/) offer for one, in bands: open it
or show it in the plex; rename, copy its path, make a note beside it; make it a
[child, a parent or a jump](/links/); change its title; ask the agent about it; remove it.

For anything else (a PDF, a picture, a file numen does not read), the menu has what can be
done to a file: rename it, copy its path, make something beside it, remove it.

If you select several rows, the menu offers only what applies to all of them, which is removing
them.

## Making text from a file

A [recording](/recordings/) and a [scanned book](/documents/#scanned-books) are transcribed by
plugins rather than from their row. A recording with no transcript offers **Transcribe** in its
own tab, under the player; a scan is read by the Document OCR plugin automatically. Both are
in the [commands](/commands/) under **File** for the open file, each labelled with the
plugin that runs it. Both run on your own machine, in the background, and show their progress in the title bar.

**Remove** puts a file in `.trash/` inside the vault, the same as anywhere else in numen.
Nothing is destroyed.

## Carrying notes onto the map

Drag notes out of the tree and over the rest of the window. Drop them
into a relation in the [plex](/plex/) (above, below, or to the side of a node) and they are
**linked** to that note, as parents, children or jumps.

Only notes can be dragged this way. A picture or a PDF among the selected rows is not moved,
because links in the vault connect notes only.
