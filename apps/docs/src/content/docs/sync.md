---
title: Numen Sync subscription
description: How to cancel, change your card or get an invoice for Numen Sync, with the app or without it, and what happens to your notes when the paid time ends.
---

Numen Sync keeps your vaults the same on all your computers. It is a subscription. Payments are handled by Creem, which sells it to you and sends you the receipt. Cancelling, changing your card and getting invoices are done on Creem's page for customers, called the customer portal.

## With the app

Open the settings, go to **Identity and licenses**, and press **Manage subscription** on the card **Numen Sync**. The customer portal opens in your browser. Select your subscription there to cancel it, change your card or get an invoice.

## Without the app

You do not need Numen to cancel. If you lost your device or cannot open the app, use the email you bought with.

1. Find the receipt Creem emailed you after a payment. Use the link in it to open the customer portal.
2. If you cannot find the receipt, open `https://creem.io/my-orders/login` in any browser and ask for a login link. Enter the email you bought with. The link comes to that inbox.
3. In the portal, select your subscription, press **Manage Subscription**, then **Cancel Subscription**.

The portal needs no password. The login link may not arrive if you enter a different email than the one you paid with. If it does not, check your spam folder and try the other emails you use.

## Refunds

You can ask for your money back within 14 days of paying. Ask Creem: the portal has a section for support and invoices, where you can copy the details of your payment and write to them.

## When the paid time ends

Cancelling stops further payments. Your notes are not removed at once:

- Until the end of the period you paid for, and 7 days after it, sync works as usual, and changes from all your computers are sent.
- After that, the vaults in the cloud are **Read only**. You can still read them, and changes stay on the computer where you made them. Nothing on your computers is deleted.
- 60 days after the end of the paid period, if you have not paid again, the cloud deletes the vaults. The notes on your computers stay.

Paying again at any point before the deletion brings sync back.

## Sharing a folder by a link

You can let another person read one folder of a synced vault, or the whole vault: right-click the vault itself to share all of it. A folder cannot be shared while its folder or the vault is shared, and the vault cannot be shared while one of its folders is shared; the message names the share in the way. They read it; they cannot change it, and nothing else of your vault reaches them. Both of you need Numen Sync with the same sync server, and you must stay online until they have pasted the link.

1. **Make the link.** Right-click the folder, choose **Share**, and copy the link. It works once and stops working after 24 hours at most; the sync server may end it sooner (today after about 10 minutes), and you can revoke it before.
2. **Send it, and let them paste it.** The other person chooses **Accept a shared folder...** and pastes the link. Both of your screens show a six-digit code.
3. **Compare the codes and allow.** If the code is the same on both screens, choose **Allow**. If it differs, choose **Decline**: someone else may be on the line. After you allow, the folder appears under **Shared with you** for the other person.

The link holds no key to your notes. A link that leaks before it is used cannot add anybody without your answer, and a second person who pastes it is refused. Remove the access of a device, or stop sharing a folder, under **Shared folders** in the settings of Numen Sync.

## Stopping the sync of a vault, or leaving a shared folder

To stop syncing a vault on one computer, right-click the vault folder in the file tree and choose **Stop syncing this vault**, or open the card **Synced vaults**, press **...** on its row and choose **Stop syncing**. To leave a folder another person shares, press **...** on its row under **Shared with you** and choose **Leave**. Stopping asks **Stop syncing <name>? The files stay on this device.** with **Keep the cloud copy** and **Delete the cloud copy**. The files stay on this computer either way. Keeping leaves the vault in the cloud, where the card lists it as **Not syncing**. Deleting removes the vault from the cloud for good, and every other computer that synced it stops syncing it at its next pass. The card **Synced vaults** lists every vault of your identity in the cloud with its size, such as **3.5 MB in the cloud**, also the ones this computer does not sync. On such a row, **...** and **Delete cloud copy** delete it after a question; get it back on a computer with **Get a vault from the cloud**. A left folder becomes an ordinary folder; the owner still lists your device until the owner removes its access.
